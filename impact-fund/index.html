Your application

Tell us what you would make possible.

Open to schools, small and medium businesses, creator-led communities and recruitment or people teams. Work, school or creator/business email is preferred so we can verify your organisation. Personal email addresses are welcome, but we may ask for proof of your authority if you are shortlisted. Your submission is reviewed privately; media is never published without your separate permission.

Before you write your answers: your application is judged on real screenshots (or a short video) from the Nervant app showing your team actually using it — not on written answers alone. Written-only entries without app evidence will not score well. See "Show Nervant in action" further down for exactly what to capture.

Standard evidence template

Show Nervant in action

Upload a photo, video or document showing how you or your team would use Nervant. You can combine your full story, screenshots and supporting evidence into one PDF and upload that single file. Use a hosted session, live poll, quiz, word cloud, presentation, podcast or Nervant Intelligence.

1. Show the product

Capture the screen, device or projection with a recognisable Nervant session or feature visible.

2. Show the people

Show the host, learners or team participating—answering, discussing or responding.

3. Explain the outcome

In your video or linked caption, say what the session helped you learn, decide or improve.

Suggested 30–60 second video sequence

  1. 01 Introduce your organisation and goal.
  2. 02 Show Nervant being used by your people.
  3. 03 Share the impact you want to create.

Submit one file or one video link. You may combine your entire story into a single PDF. Accepted: PDF, Word, PowerPoint, JPG, PNG, WebP, MP4 and MOV. Maximum upload size: 20MB. Do not include identifiable children or third parties unless you have permission.
